## Frequently asked questions

**Is secondary glazing allowed on a listed building in Stony Stratford?**
Almost always yes, because it is fully reversible and installed internally without altering the historic frame. Listed Building Consent is still needed in most cases, but is generally granted for well-designed slimline systems.

**How much noise reduction can I expect on the A5 or near the WCML?**
A properly specified secondary unit with a 100–150mm cavity and laminated acoustic glass typically reduces perceived traffic and rail noise by half or more (35–45 dB Rw).

**Will secondary glazing meet Part L?**
Combined with your existing primary window, a modern secondary system reliably achieves a combined U-value below 1.8 W/m²K, and often below 1.4, which meets Part L for replacement thermal elements.
