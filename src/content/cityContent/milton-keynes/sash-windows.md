## Frequently asked questions

**Will I need Listed Building Consent in Stony Stratford or Wolverton?**
If your property is listed, or on a street the conservation officer treats as prominent, yes. MK Council planning maintains a live list. For unlisted homes in the same conservation area you still normally need consent for street-facing changes, and slimline double glazing (usually 12–14mm sealed units) is the typical ceiling.

**Timber or UPVC sash — which is right?**
On a listed or conservation-area frontage, timber is often the only accepted material. On a modern MK estate build, UPVC vertical sliders halve the price and match Part L without repainting.

**Do sashes still meet current Part L?**
Yes. Modern sash units with warm-edge spacers and low-e coatings can hit Uw values of 1.2 to 1.4 W/m²K, which is compliant.
