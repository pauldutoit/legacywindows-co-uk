## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for UPVC in Milton Keynes?**
Generally no, on 1970s and 1980s estates you rely on permitted development. Stony Stratford, Wolverton, Newport Pagnell and Woburn Sands are conservation areas, so it is worth ringing MK Council planning before ordering.

**How long does an average MK job take?**
A ten-window whole-house swap typically takes two to three fitting days, plus a survey visit before that.

**Is FENSA or CERTASS enough for building control?**
Yes. Either one lets the installer self-certify against Part L and Part F, and the certificate reaches Land Registry within a few weeks.
