## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission or building regulations sign-off?**
On most MK estates, replacing an existing rear door with French doors falls under permitted development, but building control notification is compulsory. FENSA or CERTASS registered installers self-certify — no separate application needed.

**What is the difference between French doors and patio (sliding) doors?**
French doors are hinged and open outwards or inwards, giving a full unobstructed opening. Patio sliders run on tracks and are usually cheaper per metre but always leave one leaf blocking.

**Can I have a side light or top light added?**
Yes. Adding fixed side or top lights typically adds £150 to £400 per panel to the fitted price and is common on wider Milton Keynes rear elevations.
