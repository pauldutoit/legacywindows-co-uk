## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need Listed Building Consent for a bay in Stony Stratford or Wolverton?**
If your property is listed, yes. In the conservation area more generally, consent is normally needed for street-facing bay replacements, and you may be required to keep the original mullion pattern and glazing bar spacing.

**Can I replace a bay in the same day?**
On a modern replacement where the bay board and lead flashing are sound, yes — typically five to eight hours. On a full structural rebuild, expect two to three days.

**Do bay windows lose more heat than flat frames?**
They can, because of the extra frame perimeter, but modern warm-edge units and insulated bay boards mean U-values of 1.3 to 1.4 W/m²K are readily achievable.
