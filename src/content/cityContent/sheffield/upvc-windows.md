## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for UPVC windows on a Sheffield stone terrace?**
Most standard replacements are permitted development, but if your street sits inside one of Sheffield's 38 conservation areas — Nether Edge, Sharrow Vale, Broomhill and much of the Porter Valley included — you may need conservation area consent, especially for street-facing frames. Check the Sheffield City Council planning portal or ask your installer to confirm before signing.

**Typical price for a full 3-bed 1930s semi in Dore?**
