## Frequently asked questions

**Can I put UPVC sashes on a house in a Sheffield conservation area?**
It depends on the specific area's Article 4 direction. Nether Edge and Broomhill will usually insist on timber or timber-alternative composite frames on street-facing elevations. Rear elevations are generally more flexible. Your installer should submit a design for approval before ordering — good ones do this as standard.

**How much does a full timber sash restoration cost per window?**
Restoring an existing box frame (splicing sills, replacing sashes, draught-sealing, single or slim-glazed) usually runs £780 to £1,600 per window. Full replacement with new timber sashes and slim double glazing sits between £1,400 and £2,100. UPVC sash alternatives start around £780.

**Do slim double-glazed units meet Part L?**
Slim units (typically 4-6-4 or 4-8-4) generally achieve Uw values around 1.6-1.8 W/m²K. That is above the 1.4 Building Regs target, but Part L allows a "historic building" exemption when full compliance would harm character — Sheffield planners accept this on conservation streets.
