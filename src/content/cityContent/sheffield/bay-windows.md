## Frequently asked questions

**Can I replace a bay without touching the roof?**
Usually yes — most Sheffield Edwardian and 1930s bays have a small tiled or slated bay roof carried on separate posts. A competent installer will replace the frames while retaining the roof, provided the timber is sound. If the bay is taking upper-floor load (rare but not unheard of on 1930s semis), you need a structural engineer sign-off before removing the old frames.

**How much for a full three-panel bay?**
A standard UPVC three-panel splayed bay comes in around £1,400 to £2,400 fitted. A larger four or five-panel bow window or a heritage-style timber replacement can reach £2,800 to £3,300, especially with reinforced corner posts and lead-effect glazing bars.

**Are decorative astragal bars still available in Edwardian patterns?**
Yes. Most Sheffield installers on the panel offer applied Georgian bars, Edwardian upper-light patterns and integral leaded lights — matching what is already there is usually essential in conservation streets like Sharrow Vale.
