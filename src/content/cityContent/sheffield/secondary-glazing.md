## Frequently asked questions

**Will secondary glazing get past Listed Building Consent in Sheffield?**
Almost always yes — because it is fully reversible and does not alter the original window. Sheffield's conservation officers generally treat secondary glazing as a preferred solution over replacement. You still need to apply for consent on a listed building, but approvals are typically straightforward when the specification uses slim aluminium frames.

**Does it actually cut noise from Ecclesall Road or the Parkway?**
Yes. A 100mm air gap between the primary and secondary pane with 6.4mm acoustic laminate glass typically reduces external noise by 45-50dB — enough to make Ecclesall Road, Abbeydale Road and the A61 corridor liveable at night.

**Typical price per window?**
Standard horizontal-slider secondary units come in around £310 to £630 supplied and fitted per window; larger vertical-lift or curved units, or acoustic laminate upgrades, reach £700 to £830.
