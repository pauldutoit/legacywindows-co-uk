## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent in Manchester?**
Usually yes on a Grade II listed building, though Manchester City Council heritage officers recognise it as reversible and low-impact. The application is far simpler than replacing the primary casement. Installers in our network prepare the drawings and heritage statement.

**Will secondary glazing meet Part L on an Ancoats mill conversion?**
Listed mill conversions are largely exempt from Part L where full compliance would harm the historic fabric. Secondary glazing typically brings the combined unit down to around Uw 1.7 W/m²K, which Historic England treats as the accepted heritage benchmark.

**How much noise reduction can I expect above the Mancunian Way?**
A 100mm cavity between the primary window and a 6.4mm acoustic laminate secondary pane delivers around 45dB reduction. On a fifth-floor Deansgate flat that drops traffic noise from disruptive to a low background hum.
