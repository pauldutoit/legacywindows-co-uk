## Frequently asked questions

**Do sash windows in Victoria Park need Listed Building Consent?**
Victoria Park is a conservation area with several individually listed villas. On non-listed properties, like-for-like replacement in matching materials is permitted development. On listed properties, Listed Building Consent is needed even for internal cord replacement in some cases.

**Should I refurbish or replace an original Didsbury sash?**
Where the timber is sound and the cord, weight and beads have failed, refurbishment plus slim double glazing keeps the original glass and joinery. Manchester joiners quote this at 40 to 60 per cent of a full replacement.

**Are UPVC vertical sliders acceptable in Whalley Range?**
Non-listed properties outside the strictest streets can usually be swapped to UPVC vertical sliders, though a timber-effect foil finish is a stronger planning argument. Manchester City Council heritage officers publish street-by-street guidance.
