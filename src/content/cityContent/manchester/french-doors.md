## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need a Building Notice in Manchester?**
Replacing a door on an existing opening is covered by FENSA or CERTASS self-certification. Widening the opening or removing a load-bearing wall requires a Building Notice submitted to Manchester City Council building control, with structural calculations for the new lintel.

**Do Ancoats listed mill conversions restrict French door options?**
Yes: many Ancoats and Northern Quarter mills are Grade II listed, and the original cast-iron window grid is a protected feature. Steel-look aluminium French doors with slim mullions are usually the only permitted upgrade, and consent is required.

**How does aluminium compare to UPVC on a Chorlton back extension?**
Aluminium slim frames give a larger glazed area on wider openings above 2.4m and match the aesthetic architects usually specify on new side-return extensions. UPVC French doors give better value on narrower openings under 1.8m.
