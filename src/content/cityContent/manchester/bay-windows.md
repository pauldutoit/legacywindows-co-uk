## Frequently asked questions

**Does replacing a bay in Didsbury Village need planning permission?**
Didsbury Village is a conservation area. Like-for-like replacement in matching materials is usually permitted development, but a change of frame material or glazing pattern requires a planning application to Manchester City Council.

**Why do bay quotes vary so much on Manchester Victorian terraces?**
A splayed three-facet bay with a tiled or leaded roof, structural cill support and lead flashings is a genuinely different job from a flat casement swap. Where roof and cill are being renewed together, budget the upper end of the range.

**Can I get slim-cavity double glazing on a Chorlton bay?**
Yes: 14mm slim units with a warm-edge spacer meet the current Part L target of Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K on most bay geometries, and match the narrow sightlines that conservation officers expect on Edwardian bay-fronts.
