## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need LBC for a Hyndland tenement sash replacement?**
Almost always yes if the block is B-listed or sits in the Glasgow West Conservation Area. Timber sash-and-case with slim double-glazed units is the typical approved specification. Your installer should submit drawings and glazing details before ordering.

**Restore existing sashes or replace them entirely?**
Draught-sealing sound existing sashes runs £350 to £560 per window and cuts heat loss by around 40%. Full timber sash-and-case replacement with slim units is £1,200 to £2,110 per window. Many tenement owners in Shawlands and Hyndland restore fronts and replace rears in phases.

**Do slim units meet Section 6?**
Slim 11-14mm units achieve around Uw 1.7 W/m²K, slightly above the 1.4 target. Section 6 permits relaxation for listed and conservation buildings when full compliance would harm character — this is routinely granted in Glasgow's B-listed streets.
