## Frequently asked questions

**Can I fit French doors on a B-listed tenement rear elevation?**
Sometimes — rears often have more latitude than fronts, but LBC is still required. Timber-framed doors matched to existing window profiles are the usual approved spec. Approval is more likely when the doors face an internal court rather than a public street.

**French, patio or bifolds for a typical Newton Mearns extension?**
Openings under 2m suit French doors with fixed side lights. 2.0-2.4m works for two-panel sliding patio doors. Above 3m — common in Newton Mearns and Bearsden kitchen-diner projects — aluminium bifolds in three or four panels are the norm. Sightlines and threshold height belong in every quote.

**Typical Glasgow prices?**
UPVC French doors £1,150 to £1,800 fitted; aluminium sliders £1,900 to £2,500; three-panel aluminium bifolds £2,400 to £2,820 depending on frame colour and glass. Timber French sets for LBC jobs push toward £2,200 upwards.
