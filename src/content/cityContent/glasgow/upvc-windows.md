## Frequently asked questions

**Can I put UPVC on a West End tenement in Hyndland or Dowanhill?**
Usually no on street-facing elevations. Much of Glasgow West is a conservation area with B-listing common — timber sash-and-case or slim-glazed equivalents are typically required on fronts. Rear elevations facing an internal drying green sometimes accept UPVC, but always check with the council planning portal first.

**How does Section 6 differ from English Part L?**
Scottish Building Standards Section 6 sets similar targets (replacement windows must reach roughly Uw 1.4 W/m²K) but has different ventilation and safety-glass rules. Notification is via a Certificate of Construction under a Scheme Provider, not FENSA — although some dual-accredited installers offer both.

**Typical prices for a Bearsden 1930s semi?**
A full 8-10 window replacement usually lands £3,900 to £7,100 fitted. Individual apertures are £400 to £790 depending on size and glass spec — the upper end covers triple-glazed A++ units with security glass.
