## Frequently asked questions

**Does replacing a bay window need planning permission in a London conservation area?**
Like-for-like replacement in matching materials is usually permitted development, but Article 4 boroughs (parts of Islington, Hackney and Camden) strip that right and require a full planning application even for identical replacements.

**Why do bay window quotes vary so much on a Victorian London terrace?**
A splayed three-facet bay with a tiled or leaded roof, structural cill support and lead flashings is a different job from a flat replacement casement. Where roof and cill are being renewed at the same time, budget the upper end of the range.

**Can I get a slim-cavity double glazed bay in a Wandsworth conservation area?**
Yes: 14mm slim units with a warm-edge spacer meet the current Part L target of Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K on most bay geometries, and match the original glazing-bar sightlines that conservation officers ask for.
