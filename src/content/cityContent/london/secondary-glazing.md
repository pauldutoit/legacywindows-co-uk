## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent in London?**
Usually yes for a Grade I or II listed property, though most local authorities recognise it as reversible and low-impact. The application is far simpler than replacing the primary sash. Installers in our network prepare the drawings as part of the quote.

**Will secondary glazing meet Part L on a listed London flat?**
Listed buildings are largely exempt from Part L when full compliance would harm the historic fabric. Secondary glazing typically gets the combined unit down to around Uw 1.7 W/m²K, which is the accepted heritage benchmark.

**How much noise reduction can I expect on a busy Camden street?**
A 100mm cavity between the primary sash and a 6.4mm acoustic laminate secondary pane delivers around 45dB reduction, enough to make Camden Road or Euston Road audible only as a distant hum.
