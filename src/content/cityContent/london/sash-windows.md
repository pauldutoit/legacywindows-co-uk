## Frequently asked questions

**Do Victorian sash windows in Islington need timber replacements?**
On any street inside the borough's Article 4 Direction, yes: UPVC and aluminium replacements are almost never approved on the front elevation. Rear elevations are usually less restrictive, but check the planning portal before ordering.

**Can original sashes be refurbished rather than replaced?**
Very often, yes. Where the frame timber is sound, draught-proofing brushes, new cords, parting beads and slim double-glazed panes can bring a 120-year-old sash up to a Uw around 1.6 W/m²K without touching the outward appearance.

**What is the typical lead time on a bespoke timber sash for a London property?**
Six to twelve weeks from survey to install for made-to-measure timber, versus three to five weeks for a UPVC vertical slider from a stock profile.
