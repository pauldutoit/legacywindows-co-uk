## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need Building Regs approval in London?**
Replacing an existing door on a like-for-like opening is covered by FENSA or CERTASS self-certification. Widening the opening or removing a load-bearing wall triggers a Building Notice with your borough and structural calculations for the new lintel.

**What is the difference between French doors, patio sliders and bifolds for London use?**
French doors are hinged pairs suited to narrow Victorian openings, patio sliders give clear glass on wider openings without swing space, and bifolds fully retract but need track depth and door-stacking room. Cost order for a 2.4m opening is typically French < patio < bifold.

**Are aluminium doors overkill for a London garden extension?**
Aluminium slim frames maximise glazed area on a wide opening and match the sightlines architects specify on side returns. On narrower Victorian openings under 1.8m, UPVC French doors usually offer the better price-to-performance ratio.
