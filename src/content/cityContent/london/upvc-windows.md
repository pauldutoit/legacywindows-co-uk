## Frequently asked questions

**Are UPVC windows allowed in London conservation areas?**
Often yes at the rear, sometimes yes at the front with a woodgrain foil, occasionally not at all if an Article 4 Direction is in force. Your borough planning portal states the rule for your street. Installers in our network will not quote a scheme that would breach it.

**What Uw rating should a London installer quote for 2026?**
Building Regulations Part L requires a whole-window Uw of no more than 1.4 W/m²K on replacement work. Most London quotes we see come in at 1.2 W/m²K on A-rated units, which meets the standard comfortably.

**How long does a typical UPVC install take on a London terrace?**
A five-window Victorian terrace in N7 or SE15 is usually fitted in one to two working days, subject to parking and any scaffold hire on street-facing bays.
