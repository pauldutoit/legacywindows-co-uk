## Frequently asked questions

**Does replacing a Headingley bay need planning permission?**
Headingley sits inside a Leeds City Council conservation area with an Article 4 Direction on many streets. Like-for-like replacement in matching stone-effect materials is usually acceptable, but a change of frame material or facet count normally requires consent.

**Why do bay window quotes vary on a Roundhay villa?**
A splayed three or four-facet bay with a leaded or tiled roof, stone mullions and structural cill support is a genuinely different job from a flat casement swap. Where the stone cill needs indenting or the roof needs re-tiling, budget the upper end of the range.

**Can I get slim-cavity double glazing on a Meanwood gritstone bay?**
Yes: 14mm slim units on a warm-edge spacer meet the current Part L target of Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K on most bay geometries, and keep the narrow sightlines that conservation officers expect on gritstone-mullioned bays.
