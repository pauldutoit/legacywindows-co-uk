**Can I install UPVC on a Leeds back-to-back in a conservation area?**
Some Harehills and Chapel Allerton streets fall inside Leeds City Council conservation areas. Like-for-like replacement in matching cill and lintel detail is usually accepted, but a front-elevation change from timber sash to UPVC casement may need a full application.

**What is a fair price for a full-house UPVC replacement on a Leeds back-to-back?**
Budget £1,600 to £2,800 fully fitted for a typical two-bed back-to-back with four front-elevation windows. There are no rear windows to price, so total spend is lower than a comparable through-terrace.

**Do Headingley LS6 conservation streets accept UPVC?**
Non-listed properties outside the strictest streets usually accept a woodgrain-foil UPVC that matches the original sash proportions. Leeds City Council heritage officers publish street-level guidance and Article 4 zones.
