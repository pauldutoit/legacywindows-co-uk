## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent in Leeds?**
Usually yes on a Grade II listed building, though Leeds City Council heritage officers recognise it as reversible and low-impact. The application is simpler than replacing the primary sash. Installers in our network prepare the drawings and heritage statement as part of the quote.

**Will secondary glazing satisfy Part L on a listed Meanwood villa?**
Listed buildings are largely exempt from Part L where full compliance would harm the historic fabric. Secondary glazing typically brings the combined unit down to around Uw 1.7 W/m²K, the accepted heritage benchmark used by Historic England and mirrored in Leeds guidance.

**How much noise reduction can I expect on a Leeds inner-ring-road flat?**
A 100mm cavity between the primary sash and a 6.4mm acoustic laminate secondary pane delivers around 45dB reduction, enough to drop the A64 or A58 from a constant hum to a quiet background.
