## Frequently asked questions

**Do sash windows in Headingley need Listed Building Consent?**
Only if the property is individually listed. Most Headingley sash houses sit inside a conservation area rather than a listed designation, so like-for-like replacement in matching materials is permitted development. Article 4 restrictions apply on some streets.

**Should I refurbish or replace an original Roundhay sash?**
Where the timber is sound and only cords, brushes and beads have failed, refurbishment plus slim double glazing preserves the original glass and joinery. Leeds joiners typically quote this at 40 to 60 per cent of a full replacement.

**How do UPVC vertical sliders compare to timber in a Chapel Allerton conservation area?**
UPVC vertical sliders replicate sightlines at distance but cannot match the depth of a jointed timber cill and box frame close up. In Leeds Article 4 zones, timber is usually the safer planning choice.
