## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need a Building Notice in Leeds?**
Replacing a door on an existing opening is covered by FENSA or CERTASS self-certification. Widening the opening or removing a load-bearing wall requires a Building Notice submitted to Leeds City Council building control, with structural calculations for the new steel or masonry lintel.

**What glass spec meets Part L on a Meanwood rear extension?**
For a new extension counted under Part L1B, the door glazed-area target is Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K with a G-value that limits summer overheating. Most Leeds quotes we see come in at Uw 1.2 W/m²K on argon-filled A-rated units.

**Are bifolds worth the cost over patio sliders on a Yorkshire semi?**
Bifolds retract fully but need track depth and door-stacking room, and cost 30 to 60 per cent more than a two-pane sliding patio on the same opening. On a standard 2.4m Chapel Allerton opening, a patio slider often gives the better view-to-cost ratio.
