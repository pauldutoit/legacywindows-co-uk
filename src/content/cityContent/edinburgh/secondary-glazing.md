## Frequently asked questions

**Will Historic Environment Scotland approve secondary glazing on an A-listed New Town flat?**
Almost always yes. Because it is fully reversible and does not alter the original sash-and-case, secondary glazing is the preferred energy-efficiency route on listed Edinburgh properties. Consent is still required, but decisions are usually straightforward when slim aluminium frames matching the sash sub-divisions are used.

**How much noise does it cut from Leith Walk or Princes Street?**
A 100-150mm air gap combined with 6.4mm acoustic laminate glass typically reduces external noise by 45-50dB — enough to make Leith Walk, Lothian Road and even Princes Street bedroom facades genuinely quiet at night.

**Typical prices for a Georgian New Town sash?**
Standard horizontal-slider units £335 to £680 supplied and fitted per window; taller New Town vertical-lift units £700 to £900. Acoustic laminate adds around £120-£180 per window. A full flat with six sashes usually comes in £2,600 to £4,200 total.
