## Frequently asked questions

**Can I fit French doors on a listed tenement rear elevation?**
Sometimes — rear elevations often have more latitude than fronts, but you still need Listed Building Consent for A or B-listed properties. Timber French doors matched to existing window profiles are usually the only permitted route. Your installer should submit the design before ordering.

**French doors, patio or bifolds — which suits a typical rear extension?**
Openings of 1.5-1.8m suit French doors with fixed side lights. 2.0-2.4m suits two-panel sliding patio doors, which give a wider clear pass. Above 3m — common in modern Corstorphine and Liberton kitchen-diner extensions — three or four-panel aluminium bifolds are the norm. Sightlines and threshold height should be in the written quote.

**Typical Edinburgh prices?**
UPVC French doors £1,250 to £1,900 fitted; aluminium sliders £2,100 to £2,700; aluminium three-panel bifolds £2,600 to £3,050. Timber French doors for listed jobs push £2,400 upwards, driven mainly by joinery labour.
