## Frequently asked questions

**Can I put UPVC on a house in the New Town or Old Town?**
Generally no. The UNESCO World Heritage Site covers the New Town, Old Town and Dean Village, and virtually every street-facing window there falls under Listed Building Consent. Timber-effect UPVC has occasionally been approved on rear elevations, but never street-facing. If your building is A or B-listed anywhere in the city, expect the same answer.

**How is Edinburgh different from English cities for Building Regs?**
Scotland uses the Scottish Building Standards Technical Handbook (Section 6) rather than English Part L. The energy targets are broadly comparable (Uw 1.4 for replacements), but ventilation and safety-glass rules differ. Your installer notifies via Certification of Construction, not FENSA, unless they hold dual accreditation.

**Typical prices for a Corstorphine 1930s bungalow?**
A full replacement of 8-10 bungalow windows usually lands £4,200 to £7,500 fitted. Individual apertures come in £430 to £860 depending on size, opener count and glass spec.
