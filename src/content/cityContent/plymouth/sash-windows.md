## Frequently asked questions

**Are Plymouth Barbican sashes usually listed?**
Many are — the Barbican has one of the highest listed-building densities in the South West outside Bath. Any window change on a listed property, whether Grade II or higher, needs Listed Building Consent from Plymouth City Council before work begins.

**How often does sash paintwork need redoing near the coast?**
Coastal exposure typically cuts a paint cycle from eight to ten years down to four to six years. Accoya frames extend it back towards a decade because the timber is dimensionally stable and resists salt-driven movement.

**Can slim double glazing be fitted to a Devonport sash?**
Usually yes, if the sash box has enough depth to accept a 12-14mm sealed unit. Conservation officers at Plymouth City Council generally accept slim units when glazing bars and horns are matched to the original profile.
