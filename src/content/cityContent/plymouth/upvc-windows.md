## Frequently asked questions

**Do UPVC frames cope with Plymouth's salt air?**
UPVC itself is unaffected by salt spray, but standard steel hinges and screws can corrode within a decade near the coast. Installers we shortlist for PL1 and PL4 postcodes specify marine-grade stainless hardware and higher-grade weatherseals as standard.

**Is Devonport a conservation area?**
Parts of Devonport, particularly around Mount Wise and the surviving Georgian and Victorian streets, sit within the Stonehouse and Devonport conservation areas. UPVC on street-facing elevations can be refused there — always check with Plymouth City Council before ordering.

**How long does a full replacement take on a Plymstock semi?**
A typical three-bed semi is complete in two to three days once frames are made to measure. Lead times from survey to install usually run four to six weeks. Scaffolding is rarely needed for a two-storey install.
