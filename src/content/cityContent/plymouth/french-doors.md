## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need planning permission in Plymouth?**
For most unlisted homes outside conservation areas, replacing a window or door with a French door pair falls under permitted development. Barbican, Devonport and Stonehouse conservation properties often need consent from Plymouth City Council; check the planning portal first.

**How do French doors handle coastal wind loads?**
A properly specified French door pair with weather-rated seals and a low-threshold water bar copes with Plymouth's typical exposure. On exposed elevations facing the Sound, aluminium frames outperform UPVC on wind deflection.

**Is a low threshold worth the extra cost?**
Part M compliant thresholds add roughly £100 to £200 to a quote but make wheelchair, pram and mobility access easier. On coastal properties, a slightly raised weather bar is often specified to keep wind-driven rain out.
