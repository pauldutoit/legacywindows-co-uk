## Frequently asked questions

**Do Plymouth bay windows always need a new lintel?**
Not always. If the original steel or timber lintel is sound, it can usually be retained. Corner posts carrying part of the load above are the more common concern on Victorian bays in Mutley and Mannamead.

**Does salt air damage the roof lead on a pitched bay?**
Lead is largely inert to salt spray, but the fixings holding tiles and slates to the substrate can corrode. A good installer replaces any suspect fixings during the bay refit rather than leaving them for future failure.

**Can I keep the original bay stonework?**
Yes, in most cases. A sympathetic replacement fits the new frame inside the existing masonry opening, retaining cills and mullions where they are structurally sound. This is common in the Peverell and Mannamead conservation areas.
