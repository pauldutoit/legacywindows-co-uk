## Frequently asked questions

**Is Listed Building Consent needed on Plymouth Barbican homes?**
Usually not for internal secondary glazing, because the alteration is reversible and does not affect the external fabric. Plymouth City Council conservation officers occasionally require consent on Grade I or Grade II* buildings, so a pre-installation check is worthwhile.

**Does secondary glazing help with condensation near the coast?**
Condensation often shifts to the outer pane once secondary is fitted, because room-side air no longer meets a cold single-glazed surface. Trickle ventilation on the outer sash keeps the cavity dry and reduces mould risk.

**How much noise reduction is realistic?**
A 4-6mm laminated acoustic pane inside a 100mm cavity typically delivers 40-45 dB reduction on typical street noise, taking a busy road down to library-level indoors. Standard 4mm float glass gives around 30 dB.
