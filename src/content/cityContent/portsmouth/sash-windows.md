## Frequently asked questions

**Can I install slim double-glazed sashes in Old Portsmouth?**
Usually yes, provided the glazing bar layout, horns and profile match the original. Portsmouth City Council's conservation team generally accepts slim units at 12 to 14 mm on Georgian frontages. Full 24 mm units on the street elevation are almost always refused.

**Is Listed Building Consent required for sash restoration?**
Yes if the building is listed, whether the work is inside or outside. In practice a like-for-like restoration with matched glass and profiles is usually approved. Southsea Regency terraces are the strictest, and consent typically takes six to eight weeks.

**What is the difference between spring balance and weight-and-pulley?**
Weight-and-pulley suits period restoration and preserves original detail. Spring balance is quieter, easier to service and lets the box be shallower on modern replacements. Both are offered by our matched joiners.
