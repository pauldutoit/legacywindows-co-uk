## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing work against seafront wind noise as well as traffic?**
Yes. A 100 to 150 mm cavity between the primary sash and the secondary pane, with 6 mm acoustic laminated glass on the inner unit, cuts most low-frequency wind noise as well as passing traffic. It is a common specification along Clarence Esplanade.

**Do I need Listed Building Consent for secondary glazing in Old Portsmouth?**
Usually no, because the original frame is left in place. Portsmouth City Council conservation officers often actively recommend secondary glazing on Georgian and Regency stock for exactly that reason. A quick call to the conservation team confirms whether consent is needed on your street.

**Can secondary glazing be removed cleanly if I sell the house?**
Yes. Aluminium secondary units are fitted to the surrounding reveal, not the original frame, and remove without damage to the sash.
