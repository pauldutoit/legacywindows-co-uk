## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need Listed Building Consent for sash work in Kemptown or Brunswick?**
If the building is listed, yes, for any change inside or outside. Even where the building is unlisted, Article 4 directions across parts of Brunswick and Kemptown catch every window change. Brighton and Hove City Council conservation officers commonly approve slim double-glazed replicas but refuse full 24 mm units on street elevations.

**Can I keep the original box and just replace the sashes?**
Often the box is worth saving. Refurbishing the box, fitting new draught-proofed sashes with slim units and adding modern balances typically runs 30 to 40 per cent cheaper than a full box replacement.

**What acoustic performance do slim units deliver on the seafront?**
Slim units alone cut noise by around 25 dB. For seafront elevations we usually pair slim glazing with a secondary unit to reach 40 dB or higher.
