**Is UPVC allowed on Brighton's Regency streets?**
Not on the front elevations. Kemptown, Brunswick, Montpelier and much of Hove sit in strict conservation areas covering thirty-four zones across the city, and Regency frontages usually require timber sash or a slim aluminium equivalent. Rear elevations and side returns are often more relaxed.

**How does salt air affect UPVC in Brighton?**
The frame itself is untouched, but the hardware corrodes if it is not marine grade. Every quote on our Brighton panel specifies stainless steel A4 hinges and cylinders because chromed hardware pits within a couple of winters.

**Do I need permission on an Article 4 street?**
Yes. Article 4 directions in parts of Hanover and Preston Park remove permitted development rights for window changes, so a planning application is required even for a like-for-like replacement. Brighton and Hove City Council responds to most applications inside eight weeks.
