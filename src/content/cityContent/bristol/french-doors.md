## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need a Building Notice in Bristol?**
Replacing a door on an existing opening is covered by FENSA or CERTASS self-certification. Widening the opening or removing a load-bearing wall requires a Building Notice submitted to Bristol City Council building control, with structural calculations for the new lintel.

**Can I fit bifolds on a Clifton rear elevation?**
Rear elevations are often less restrictive than the front in Clifton, but the property still sits inside a conservation area. Timber or slim aluminium bifolds are usually approved; UPVC bifolds are rarely accepted. Listed properties require Listed Building Consent even for rear-elevation changes.

**How does aluminium compare to UPVC on a Henleaze extension?**
Aluminium slim frames maximise glazed area on wider openings above 2.4m and match the aesthetic architects usually specify on new side-return extensions. UPVC French doors give better value on narrower openings under 1.8m on standard 1930s semis.
