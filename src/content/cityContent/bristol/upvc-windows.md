## Frequently asked questions

**Can I install UPVC on a Clifton Victorian property?**
Clifton, Kingsdown and Cliftonwood are among Bristol's strictest conservation areas. UPVC is very rarely permitted on the front elevation of a Clifton townhouse; rear elevations sometimes accept a woodgrain-foil finish. Bristol City Council heritage officers publish street-level guidance.

**What is a fair price for a full-house UPVC replacement on a Bedminster terrace?**
Budget £2,500 to £4,200 fully fitted for a typical BS3 two-bed bay-fronted terrace with a three-facet bay downstairs, two upstairs casements, kitchen and landing windows at 2026 South West rates.

**Do Southville bay-fronts need matching leaded lights?**
Where the original upper lights are leaded, a like-for-like replacement is usually expected outside conservation areas as a matter of design, though not always as a legal requirement. Installers in our network offer matching lead-effect double-glazed units at a modest premium over plain glass.
