## Frequently asked questions

**Do Clifton sashes need Listed Building Consent?**
Very often yes: Clifton has one of the densest concentrations of listed Georgian buildings outside London, and the whole area is a conservation zone. Any change to a street-facing sash on a listed property requires Listed Building Consent from Bristol City Council.

**Can I put slim double glazing in a Kingsdown Georgian sash?**
Yes, on non-listed properties inside the conservation area, and often on listed properties too where the glazing bar profile is preserved. Slim 14mm units on a warm-edge spacer maintain the original slim astragal appearance.

**Should I refurbish or replace a Redland Victorian sash?**
Where the timber is sound and cords, brushes and beads have failed, refurbishment plus slim double glazing preserves the original glass and joinery. Bristol joiners quote this at 40 to 60 per cent of a full replacement, and it is often the better answer for a bay-fronted Redland terrace.
