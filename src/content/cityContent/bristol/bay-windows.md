## Frequently asked questions

**Does replacing a Clifton bay need Listed Building Consent?**
Very often yes: the majority of Clifton bay-fronted townhouses are Grade II listed and any change to a street-facing bay requires Listed Building Consent from Bristol City Council. Rear-elevation bays sometimes have more flexibility, but consent is still normally needed.

**Why do bay quotes vary on a Bedminster Victorian terrace?**
A three-facet bay with a tiled or leaded roof, structural cill support and lead flashings is a different job from a flat casement swap. Where the bay roof needs re-leading or the cill needs indenting, budget the upper end of the range.

**Can I get slim-cavity double glazing on a Southville bay?**
Yes: 14mm slim units on a warm-edge spacer meet the current Part L target of Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K on most bay geometries, and match the narrow sightlines that Bristol conservation officers expect on Victorian bay-fronts.
