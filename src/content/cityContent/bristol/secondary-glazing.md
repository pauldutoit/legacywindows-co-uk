## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent in Clifton?**
Usually yes on a Grade II listed Clifton townhouse, though Bristol City Council heritage officers recognise internal secondary units as reversible and low-impact. The application is far simpler than replacing the primary sash. Installers in our network prepare the drawings and heritage statement.

**Will secondary glazing satisfy Part L on a listed Bristol property?**
Listed buildings are largely exempt from Part L where full compliance would harm the historic fabric. Secondary glazing typically brings the combined unit down to around Uw 1.7 W/m²K, the accepted heritage benchmark used by Historic England and mirrored in Bristol guidance.

**How much noise reduction can I expect on a Whiteladies Road flat?**
A 100mm cavity between the primary sash and a 6.4mm acoustic laminate secondary pane delivers around 45dB reduction, enough to drop Whiteladies Road or the Portway from a constant hum to a quiet background.
