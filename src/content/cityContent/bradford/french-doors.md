## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission or building regulations sign-off?**
On rear elevations outside conservation areas, replacing a door with French doors is permitted development, but building control notification is compulsory. FENSA or CERTASS installers self-certify.

**French doors or bifold — which suits Bradford homes?**
French doors work well on the standard 1500mm and 1800mm openings of Victorian terraces and interwar semis. Bifolds need a 2.4m+ opening and are more common on modern rear extensions.

**Can Yorkshire-stone reveals fit standard door frames?**
Yes, but they often need custom aluminium or timber cill trims for a weather-tight finish. Bradford-based fitters price this into their surveys.
