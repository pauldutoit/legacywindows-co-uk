## Frequently asked questions

**Is a bay replacement structural work?**
Sometimes. If the bay board or wall plate above the frames is load-bearing (common on two-storey Victorian villas), a structural engineer's calculation is needed and Bradford building control will inspect. FENSA registration does not cover that element.

**How long does an average bay job take?**
On a like-for-like replacement with a sound bay board, five to eight hours. With structural work or lead flashing, one to three days.

**Will a bay meet Part L?**
Yes. Modern warm-edge units and insulated bay boards reach Uw values of 1.3 to 1.4 W/m²K — compliant with current Part L for replacement windows.
