## Frequently asked questions

**Will I need Listed Building Consent in Saltaire?**
Almost certainly yes, and Bradford Council's conservation officer usually restricts you to timber sash matching the original glazing bar pattern, with slimline double-glazed units of around 12–14mm. UPVC is not accepted on street-facing elevations in Saltaire.

**Timber sash — how long does it last?**
Modern engineered timber (typically Accoya or laminated softwood) with factory paint finish carries 30-year decorative warranties. Traditional softwood needs repainting every 8–12 years to keep water out.

**Do timber sashes meet Part L?**
Yes. With warm-edge spacers and low-e glazing, slimline sash units reach Uw values of 1.2 to 1.4 W/m²K — fully compliant even in Grade II listed buildings, subject to conservation officer sign-off.
