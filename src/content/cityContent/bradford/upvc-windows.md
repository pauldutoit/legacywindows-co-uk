## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for UPVC in Bradford?**
Generally no, on interwar and postwar stock you rely on permitted development. Conservation areas such as Saltaire, Little Germany and parts of Undercliffe do restrict frame material and finish — check with Bradford Council planning.

**How does building control get satisfied?**
FENSA or CERTASS registered installers self-certify against Part L and Part F. Your certificate typically arrives within four to six weeks.

**Are Yorkshire-stone terraces harder to fit?**
Slightly. Stone reveals often need careful trim finishing and lead flashing, which most established BD fitters have priced into their surveys.
