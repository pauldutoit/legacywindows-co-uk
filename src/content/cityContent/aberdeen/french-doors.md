**Do French doors need consent in Aberdeen?**
For most unlisted homes outside conservation areas, French doors replacing an existing window or door fall under permitted development. Listed properties and West End conservation streets need consent from Aberdeen City Council under Historic Environment Scotland policy.

**How do French doors handle North Sea wind loads?**
A properly specified French door pair with weather-rated seals and a water bar copes with typical Aberdeen exposure. On coastal elevations, aluminium frames outperform UPVC on wind deflection, and multi-point locking is standard.

**Is a low threshold worth the extra cost?**
Part M-equivalent thresholds add roughly £100 to £200 to a quote but ease wheelchair, pram and mobility access. On exposed coastal elevations, a slightly raised water bar is often specified to keep wind-driven rain out.
