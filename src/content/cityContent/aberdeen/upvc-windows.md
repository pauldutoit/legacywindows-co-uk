## Frequently asked questions

**Can I fit UPVC on an Aberdeen granite tenement?**
Granite tenements in the West End and Rosemount are usually within a conservation area and often B-listed. UPVC on street-facing elevations is regularly refused; timber sash or casement replicas are the standard route. Rear elevations are sometimes easier to approve.

**What Scottish regulations apply to a window replacement?**
Section 6 of the Scottish Building Standards sets the energy performance threshold — practically the same as English Part L, with a whole-window U-value target around 1.4 W/m²K. Historic Environment Scotland listing brings additional consent requirements.

**How long does a full replacement take on a Cults bungalow?**
A typical three-bed bungalow is complete in two to three days once frames are made to measure. Lead times from survey to install run four to six weeks. Building warrant clearance is handled by the installer through Aberdeen City Council.
