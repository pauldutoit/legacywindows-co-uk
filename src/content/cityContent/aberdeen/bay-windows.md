## Frequently asked questions

**Do granite bays need special structural inspection?**
Yes. Granite solid-wall construction has no cavity, and lintels are often granite slabs that need careful inspection during frame replacement. A good installer surveys the head plate and corner posts before quoting rather than assuming.

**Can I keep the original granite mullions?**
Usually yes. A sympathetic replacement fits the new frame inside the existing masonry opening, retaining granite cills and mullions where structurally sound. This is common practice in West End and Old Aberdeen conservation areas.

**How long does a bay replacement take?**
A typical projecting or canted bay is fitted in one to two days once made to measure. Lead times from survey to install run four to seven weeks. Scaffolding is normally needed only if the bay carries a first-floor structure above.
