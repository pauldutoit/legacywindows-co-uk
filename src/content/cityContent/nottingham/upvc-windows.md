## Frequently asked questions

**Can I put UPVC on a house in The Park Estate?**
Generally no on street-facing elevations. The Park is a private conservation enclave with heavy Victorian villa stock and its own residents' association — timber or timber-effect composite is the expectation. Rear elevations sometimes have more latitude but always check with Nottingham City Council planning first.

**Are West Bridgford Victorian terraces subject to Article 4?**
Parts of Musters Road and around Trent Bridge fall under conservation area rules where street-facing window changes need approval. A local installer will know which streets these are — worth asking before you sign.

**Typical price for a Wollaton or West Bridgford semi?**
A full 8-10 window replacement usually lands £3,800 to £7,100 fitted. Individual apertures come in £400 to £800 depending on size, opener count and glass spec.
