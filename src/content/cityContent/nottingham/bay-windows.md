## Frequently asked questions

**Can I replace a West Bridgford Edwardian bay without touching the roof?**
Usually yes — most West Bridgford bays have their own small tiled bay roof carried on separate corner posts. If the bay is taking upper-floor load (rare but not unheard of), you need structural engineer sign-off before removing the old frames.

**Typical price for a Wollaton semi bay?**
A three-panel splayed UPVC bay comes in around £1,425 to £2,550 fitted. Timber-effect UPVC or full timber replacement for a Mapperley Park heritage bay runs £2,650 to £3,400. Reinforced posts and a new tiled bay roof push toward the upper end.

**Are Edwardian upper-light patterns still available?**
Yes. Most Nottingham installers on the panel offer applied Georgian bars, Edwardian upper-light patterns and integrated leaded lights — matching what is already there is essential in The Park and Mapperley Park.
