## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need LBC or conservation consent for a Park Estate sash replacement?**
Yes for anything visible from a Park road. The Park has its own governance body and the City Council conservation team is closely involved. Approved specifications are typically timber sash-and-case with slim double glazing.

**Restore or replace on a Radford Victorian terrace?**
Draught-sealing an existing sound sash runs £350 to £530 per window and cuts heat loss around 40%. Full timber sash-and-case replacement with slim units £1,250 to £2,140. UPVC sash alternatives (only where the street allows) start £800.

**Do slim units meet Part L?**
Slim 11-14mm units achieve Uw around 1.7 W/m²K, above the 1.4 target. Part L allows a "historic building" exemption when full compliance would harm character — routinely accepted in The Park and the Lace Market.
