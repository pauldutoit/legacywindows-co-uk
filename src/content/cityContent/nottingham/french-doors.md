## Frequently asked questions

**French, patio or bifolds for a typical West Bridgford extension?**
Openings under 2m suit French doors with fixed side lights. 2.0-2.4m works for two-panel sliders. Above 3m — common in West Bridgford and Wollaton kitchen-diner projects — aluminium three or four-panel bifolds are the norm. Sightlines and threshold height should be in every written quote.

**Do I need Building Control sign-off?**
Yes if you are cutting a new opening in a load-bearing wall. The lintel spec, cavity closer and thermal bridging all need to meet Part L. Your installer's quote should note whether they handle Building Control notification or whether that is your builder's responsibility.
