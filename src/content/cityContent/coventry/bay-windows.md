## Frequently asked questions

**Is Building Regulations sign-off needed for a bay replacement in Coventry?**
Yes if the bay is load-bearing, which is common on Earlsdon and Radford Victorian bays. A lintel and structural calculation is required in addition to the FENSA glazing notice.

**Can I fit UPVC on an Earlsdon bay?**
On rear elevations, usually yes. On the street elevation the conservation officer will usually require timber or a slim heritage aluminium equivalent. Chapelfields is similar.

**What is the difference between a square, splayed and canted bay?**
Square bays project at 90 degrees, splayed at around 45 degrees and canted are a shallow splay of 30 degrees. Coventry's Victorian stock tends to be square, and the interwar semis in Chapelfields and Stivichall are splayed.
