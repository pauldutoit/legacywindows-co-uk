## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need planning permission in Reading?**
Not usually if they replace an existing door or a window on a rear elevation under permitted development. Article 4 directions in parts of Caversham and the central conservation area remove those rights, so a call to Reading Borough Council planning is worth the ten minutes.

**How does Thames flood risk affect the door choice?**
For homes inside Environment Agency Flood Zone 2 or 3 the threshold detail matters. Aluminium door frames with a raised sill and a flood-resistant seal reduce the risk of water ingress. Our matched fitters can price this in for riverside RG1 and RG4 addresses.

**Are inward or outward-opening doors better on a small patio?**
Outward-opening French doors save internal floor space and suit small patios and balconies. Inward-opening doors give more flexibility with garden furniture. Both meet Part L and Part K.
