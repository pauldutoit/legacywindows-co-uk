**Do I need permission for UPVC in Caversham or central Reading?**
Reading has thirteen conservation areas including Caversham and pockets of the town centre. UPVC on rear elevations is usually fine, but street-facing frontages in the conservation zones often require timber or a slim aluminium equivalent. Article 4 directions on parts of Caversham remove permitted development rights, so a call to Reading Borough Council planning is worth the time.

**Does the Thames flood risk affect the install?**
