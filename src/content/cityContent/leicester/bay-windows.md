## Frequently asked questions

**Is Building Regulations sign-off needed for a bay replacement in Leicester?**
Yes if the bay is load-bearing, which is the case on most Clarendon Park Victorian terraces. A lintel and structural calculation is required in addition to the FENSA glazing notice.

**Can I fit UPVC on a Clarendon Park bay?**
On rear elevations, usually yes. On the street elevation the conservation officer will normally require timber or a slim heritage aluminium equivalent. Stoneygate is stricter still.

**How long does a bay window replacement take?**
A typical three-light bay is fitted in one day. Where the head is load-bearing, allow a second day for the lintel work and building control sign-off.
