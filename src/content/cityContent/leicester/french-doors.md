## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for French doors in Leicester?**
Not usually if they replace an existing door or a window on a rear elevation under permitted development. Article 4 areas across Stoneygate and Clarendon Park remove those rights, so a call to Leicester City Council planning is worth the ten minutes. Front and side elevations always merit a check.

**What is the difference between rebated and non-rebated French doors?**
Rebated doors have one leaf overlapping the other, which improves weather sealing on exposed elevations. Non-rebated pairs meet at a central closer bead and cost slightly less. Our panel quotes both where useful.

**Should the outer pane be toughened or laminated?**
Both meet Part K. Laminated glass stays intact if broken, and it is standard on our Leicester panel for ground-floor installations because it holds up against a boot better than a toughened pane.
