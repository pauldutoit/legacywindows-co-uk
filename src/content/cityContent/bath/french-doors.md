## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for rear French doors in Bath?**
On non-listed properties outside the World Heritage core, no — permitted development covers replacement of an existing rear door. Inside the Bath City Conservation Area, planning consent is normally required, and Listed Buildings always need Listed Building Consent.

**Timber or UPVC French doors on a Bathwick frontage?**
On any Listed Building or Georgian principal elevation, timber is the standard requirement. UPVC is unlikely to be approved by the BANES conservation officer.

**Are French doors safer than patio sliders?**
Both meet PAS 24 when specified to modern standards. French doors typically use multi-point locking hooks; patio sliders use anti-lift track blocks. Part Q laminated glass is standard in ground-floor positions.
