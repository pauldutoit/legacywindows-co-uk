## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for UPVC in Bath?**
Outside the World Heritage core and on non-listed stock, no — you rely on permitted development. Inside the Bath City Conservation Area or where an Article 4 direction is in force, planning permission is normally required and UPVC is rarely accepted on principal elevations.

**How does building control get satisfied?**
FENSA or CERTASS registered installers self-certify against Part L and Part F. Your certificate typically arrives within four to six weeks.

**Can I get UPVC that suits Bath stone frontages?**
On non-principal elevations, foil-finished cream, painted-white and heritage-colour UPVC is common on Weston and Combe Down 1930s semis where the frontage sits behind a Bath stone or render surround.
