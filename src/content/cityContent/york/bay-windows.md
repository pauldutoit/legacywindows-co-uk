## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need Listed Building Consent for a bay in York?**
If your property is listed, yes — always. Inside the York Central Historic Core Conservation Area on an unlisted property, planning consent is normally needed for any street-facing bay change. City of York Council's conservation officer generally requires timber, the original mullion pattern and original glazing bar spacing.

**Is a bay replacement structural work?**
Sometimes. On Victorian terraces with a load-bearing bay board, a structural engineer's calculation is required and York building control will inspect. FENSA does not cover that element.

**How long does an average bay job take?**
On a like-for-like UPVC replacement with a sound bay board, five to eight hours. A full timber conservation-approved bay with fresh flashing can take two to four days.
