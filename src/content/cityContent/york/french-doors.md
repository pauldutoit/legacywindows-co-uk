## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for rear French doors in York?**
On non-listed properties outside the historic core, no — permitted development covers replacement of an existing rear door. Inside the York Central Historic Core Conservation Area, planning consent is normally required, and Listed Buildings always need Listed Building Consent.

**Timber or UPVC French doors on a Bishophill frontage?**
On any Listed Building or historic principal elevation, timber is the standard requirement. UPVC is unlikely to be approved by the City of York Council conservation officer.

**Can I get double doors on a narrow terrace rear?**
Yes. Standard sets from 1200mm to 1800mm suit most York terrace openings, with side lights available where the opening is wider.
