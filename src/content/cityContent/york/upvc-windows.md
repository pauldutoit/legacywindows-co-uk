## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for UPVC in York?**
Outside the historic core and on non-listed stock, no — you rely on permitted development. Inside the York Central Historic Core Conservation Area, or where an Article 4 direction is in force, planning permission is normally required and UPVC is rarely accepted on principal elevations.

**How does building control get satisfied?**
FENSA or CERTASS registered installers self-certify against Part L and Part F. Your certificate typically arrives within four to six weeks.

**Can UPVC look like painted timber for York's brick terraces?**
Yes. Foil-finished cream, Chartwell green and painted-white UPVC is common on the interwar semis of Acomb and Heworth where the frontage sits behind a brick surround.
