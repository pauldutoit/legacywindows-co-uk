## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need conservation area consent for a Pontcanna sash?**
For street-facing replacements, yes. Pontcanna's Article 4 direction removes permitted development rights for windows, so any change needs Cardiff Council approval. Timber sash-and-case with slim double glazing is the typical approved specification.

**Restore or replace on a Cathays Edwardian terrace?**
Draught-sealing a sound existing sash £340 to £520 per window (cuts heat loss around 40%). Full timber sash replacement with slim units £1,200 to £2,110. UPVC sash alternatives from £790 where the street allows.

**Can original stained-glass upper lights be retained?**
Yes — most Cardiff panel installers offer to remove, restore and re-set original stained-glass or leaded upper lights into new lower sashes, keeping the terrace's character intact. This is often the sensible middle path in Roath and Pontcanna.
