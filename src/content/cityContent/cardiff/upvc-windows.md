## Frequently asked questions

**Can I put UPVC on a Pontcanna or Roath Park conservation terrace?**
Both are conservation areas with tight rules on street-facing frames. UPVC is sometimes permitted where the profile carefully replicates the Edwardian bay-front glazing bar pattern, but timber-effect composite is a safer route on street elevations. Rear elevations usually have more latitude. Always check the Cardiff Council planning portal before ordering.

**How is Wales different from England for Building Regs?**
Wales uses Part L of the Welsh Building Regulations, which broadly mirrors England but with some tighter thermal targets and specific Welsh Government policies on renewable-ready detailing. Notification is via FENSA or CERTASS as in England.

**Typical Cathays or Roath terrace price?**
A full 7-9 window replacement including a bay usually lands £3,800 to £7,100 fitted. Individual apertures come in £400 to £790 depending on size, opener count and glass spec.
