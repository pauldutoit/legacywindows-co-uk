## Frequently asked questions

**French, patio or bifolds for a typical Cardiff extension?**
Openings under 2m suit French doors with fixed side lights. 2.0-2.4m works for two-panel sliders. Above 3m — common in Cyncoed and Lisvane kitchen-diner extensions — three or four-panel aluminium bifolds are the norm. Sightlines and threshold height should be in every quote.

**Do I need Building Control sign-off?**
Yes if you are cutting a new opening in a load-bearing wall. Under Welsh Regs, the lintel spec, cavity closer and thermal bridging all need to meet Part L. Your installer's quote should note whether they handle Building Control notification or whether the builder does.

**Typical Cardiff prices?**
UPVC French doors £1,150 to £1,800 fitted; aluminium sliders £1,900 to £2,500; three-panel aluminium bifolds £2,400 to £2,820 depending on frame colour and glass. Timber sets for Pontcanna or Llandaff conservation streets push toward £2,200 upwards.
