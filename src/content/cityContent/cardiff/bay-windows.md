## Frequently asked questions

**Can I replace a Cathays bay-front without touching the roof?**
Usually yes — most Cardiff two-storey bays run the full height with the upper bay windows sitting on the lower ones, and a small tiled bay roof at the top. If the bay carries roof load (rare on standard terraces), a structural engineer sign-off is needed before removing the old frames.

**Typical Cardiff bay price?**
A standard three-panel splayed UPVC bay comes in around £1,410 to £2,600 fitted for a single-storey bay, or £2,400 to £3,340 for a full two-storey bay-front. Timber or timber-effect for a Pontcanna conservation frontage sits at the upper end.

**Are Edwardian glazing bar patterns available?**
Yes. All Cardiff panel installers offer applied Georgian bars, Edwardian upper-light patterns and integrated stained-glass replication — matching the original detail is usually essential in Pontcanna, Roath Park and Cathays Park.
