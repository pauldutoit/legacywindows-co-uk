**Can I fit UPVC on a Broughty Ferry sandstone tenement?**
Broughty Ferry sits within a conservation area and many tenements are B-listed. UPVC on street-facing elevations is often refused; timber sash-and-case replicas are the standard route. Rear elevations are sometimes easier to approve.

**What Scottish regulations apply to a window replacement?**
Section 6 of the Scottish Building Standards sets the energy performance threshold, with a whole-window U-value target around 1.4 W/m²K. Historic Environment Scotland listing brings additional consent requirements on protected buildings.
