## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent in Dundee?**
Usually no, because it is a reversible internal alteration that does not affect the historic external fabric. Dundee City Council conservation officers, applying Historic Environment Scotland policy, occasionally require consent on A-listed buildings.

**How much heat loss does secondary glazing save?**
A well-fitted secondary panel typically halves the U-value on a single-glazed sash, from around 5.4 to 2.5 W/m²K. It also cuts draughts, which matters especially in Dundee's exposed Tay Estuary climate.

**Will it stop condensation on my sash-and-case windows?**
Condensation often shifts to the outer pane once secondary is fitted, because room-side air no longer meets a cold single-glazed surface. Trickle ventilation on the outer sash keeps the cavity dry.
