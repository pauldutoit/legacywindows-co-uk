**Is Tettenhall Wood protected under Article 4?**
Tettenhall Wood is a conservation area, and parts of the surrounding streetscape are covered by Article 4 directions restricting permitted development. Any window change on affected properties requires planning permission from Wolverhampton City Council.

**How much does draught-proofing cost versus replacement?**
Draught-proofing a working sash usually costs £250 to £450 per window and cuts infiltration by 60 to 80 percent. A full painted-timber replacement typically starts around £900. Many Wolverhampton owners phase the work.

**Can slim double glazing be fitted to Victorian sashes?**
Usually yes, if the sash box has enough depth to accept a 12-14mm sealed unit. Conservation officers at Wolverhampton City Council generally accept slim units when horns and glazing bars match the original.
