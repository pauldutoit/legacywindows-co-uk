## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need planning permission in Wolverhampton?**
For most unlisted homes outside conservation areas, French doors replacing an existing window or door fall under permitted development. Chapel Ash and Tettenhall Wood conservation streets need consent from the City of Wolverhampton Council.

**What is the difference between French doors and patio doors?**
French doors open outwards or inwards as a hinged pair, giving a wide unobstructed opening. Patio doors slide horizontally on a track and take less swing space. French doors suit narrower openings; sliders suit wider spans.

**Are aluminium French doors worth the extra cost?**
Aluminium frames are slimmer, stronger and lighter than UPVC, so they suit larger openings without sagging. Expect to pay 30 to 50 percent more than an equivalent UPVC pair. On standard 1,500mm openings, UPVC is often the better value.
