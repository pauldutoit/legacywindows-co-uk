## Frequently asked questions

**Do bay windows always need a new lintel when replaced?**
Not always. If the existing steel or timber lintel is structurally sound, it can be retained. Corner posts and head plates carrying part of the load above are the more common issue on Wolverhampton bays.

**Can I convert a square bay to a bow?**
Yes, but the shape change alters external appearance, which may need planning permission in a conservation area. On unlisted homes outside protected streets, it usually falls under permitted development.

**How long does a bay replacement take?**
A typical square or canted bay is fitted in one to two days once made to measure. Lead times from survey to install run four to seven weeks. Scaffolding is normally only needed if the bay carries a first-floor structure above.
