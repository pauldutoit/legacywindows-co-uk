**Can I fit UPVC in Tettenhall Wood?**
Tettenhall Wood is one of Wolverhampton's protected conservation areas, along with Chapel Ash. UPVC is often refused on street-facing elevations of contributing properties. Check with the City of Wolverhampton Council planning portal before ordering.

**Do UPVC windows meet Part L Building Regulations?**
Yes, provided whole-window U-value is 1.4 W/m²K or better and Part F trickle vents are included where the previous windows had them. Every FENSA installer we shortlist certifies this at handover.
