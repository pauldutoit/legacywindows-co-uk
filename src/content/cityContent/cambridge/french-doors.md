## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for rear French doors in Cambridge?**
On non-listed properties outside conservation areas, no — permitted development covers replacement of an existing rear door. Inside the Central Conservation Area, planning consent is normally required, and Listed Buildings always need Listed Building Consent.

**Timber or UPVC French doors on a listed frontage?**
On any Listed Building or historic principal elevation, timber is the standard requirement. UPVC is unlikely to be approved by the Cambridge City Council conservation officer.

**Can I have side lights added?**
Yes. Fixed side or top lights typically add £180 to £450 per panel and are common on wider Victorian terrace rear openings.
