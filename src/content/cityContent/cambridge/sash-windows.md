## Frequently asked questions

**Will I need Listed Building Consent from Cambridge City Council?**
If your property is listed, yes — always. Even on unlisted buildings inside the Central Conservation Area, planning consent is normally needed for any street-facing sash change, and the conservation officer typically requires timber, matching glazing bar profiles and slim double glazing.

**Is slimline double glazing thermally effective?**
Yes. Modern 12–14mm sealed units with warm-edge spacers, low-e coatings and argon fill achieve Uw values of 1.4 to 1.6 W/m²K — compliant with Part L for replacement windows in a Listed Building context.

**How long does a bespoke timber sash quote take?**
Survey and joinery order usually take three to six weeks; installation for a full house of six to twelve sashes takes five to ten days on site.
