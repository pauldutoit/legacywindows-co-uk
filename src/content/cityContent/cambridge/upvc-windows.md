## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for UPVC in Cambridge?**
Outside conservation areas and on non-listed stock, no — you rely on permitted development. Inside the Central Conservation Area, or where an Article 4 direction is in force, planning permission is normally required and UPVC is rarely accepted on principal elevations.

**How does building control get satisfied?**
FENSA or CERTASS registered installers self-certify against Part L and Part F. Your certificate typically arrives within four to six weeks and reaches Land Registry automatically.

**Can I get UPVC that looks like timber?**
Yes. Woodgrain foils in cream, Chartwell green and painted-white finishes are common and often accepted in less-sensitive conservation-adjacent areas.
