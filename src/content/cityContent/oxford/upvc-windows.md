## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for UPVC in Oxford?**
Outside conservation areas and on non-listed stock, no — you rely on permitted development. Inside any of Oxford's 17 conservation areas, or where an Article 4 direction is in force, planning permission is normally required and UPVC is rarely accepted on principal elevations.

**How does building control get satisfied?**
FENSA or CERTASS registered installers self-certify against Part L and Part F. Your certificate typically arrives within four to six weeks.

**Can UPVC look like painted timber for Oxford stone-fronted homes?**
Yes. Foil-finished cream, painted-white and Cotswold stone colour-matched UPVC is common on 1930s Cowley and Headington semis where the frontage sits behind a stone or render surround.
