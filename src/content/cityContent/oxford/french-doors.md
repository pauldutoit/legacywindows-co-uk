## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission for rear French doors in Oxford?**
On non-listed properties outside conservation areas, no — permitted development covers replacement of an existing rear door. Inside any of Oxford's 17 conservation areas, planning consent is normally required, and Listed Buildings always need Listed Building Consent.

**Timber or UPVC French doors on a Jericho or North Oxford frontage?**
On any Listed Building or historic principal elevation, timber is the standard requirement. UPVC is unlikely to be approved by the Oxford City Council conservation officer.

**Are French doors safer than patio sliders?**
Both meet PAS 24 when supplied to a modern spec. French doors typically use multi-point locking hooks and mushroom cams; patio sliders use anti-lift track blocks. Part Q laminated glass is standard in ground-floor positions.
