## Frequently asked questions

**Do bay windows need a new lintel when replaced?**
Not always. If the existing lintel is structurally sound, it can usually be retained. Corner posts and head plates carrying part of the load above are more often the concern on Norwich Victorian terraces, and a good installer will inspect the cavity before quoting.

**Can I switch a square bay to a bow window?**
Yes, but it changes the external appearance, which may need planning permission in a conservation area. On unlisted homes outside protected streets it usually falls under permitted development.

**How long does a bay replacement take?**
A typical square or canted bay is fitted in one to two days once made to measure. Lead times from survey to install run four to seven weeks. Scaffolding is normally needed only if the bay has a first-floor structure above.
