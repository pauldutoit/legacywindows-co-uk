**Can I fit UPVC windows in the Norwich Golden Triangle?**
The Golden Triangle sits partly within the city-centre conservation area and includes many locally listed streets. UPVC is often refused on street-facing elevations; rear or side windows are usually easier to approve. Check with Norwich City Council planning before ordering.

**Do I need Listed Building Consent for a rear window replacement?**
If the property is listed at any grade, Listed Building Consent is required for any window change, front or rear. Article 4 directions in some conservation areas further restrict permitted development. A local installer can advise on the paperwork.
