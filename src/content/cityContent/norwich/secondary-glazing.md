## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent in Norwich?**
Usually no, because it is a reversible internal alteration that does not affect the original fabric. That said, Norwich City Council conservation officers can require consent on Grade I or Grade II* buildings, so a quick check before ordering is worthwhile.

**How much does secondary glazing improve heat loss?**
A well-fitted secondary panel typically halves U-value on a single-glazed sash, taking it from around 5.4 to 2.5 W/m²K. It also cuts draughts, which often matters more day-to-day than the U-value figure alone.

**Will it stop condensation on my sash windows?**
Secondary glazing usually shifts condensation onto the outer pane rather than eliminating it, because the room-side air is now separated from the cold surface. Trickle ventilation on the outer window helps keep the cavity dry.
