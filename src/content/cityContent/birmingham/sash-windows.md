## Frequently asked questions

**Do sash windows in Edgbaston need Listed Building Consent?**
Only if the building itself is listed. Most Edgbaston sashes sit inside a conservation area rather than on a listed property, so like-for-like replacement in matching materials is permitted development, though Article 4 Directions apply on some streets.

**Should I refurbish or replace an original Moseley sash?**
Where the timber is sound and only cords, brushes and beads have failed, refurbishment plus slim double glazing preserves the original glass and joinery and typically costs 40 to 60 per cent of a full replacement.

**How does a UPVC vertical slider compare to a timber sash on a Harborne street?**
UPVC vertical sliders replicate the sightlines from a distance but cannot match the depth and shadow of a jointed timber cill and box frame at close range. On conservation streets, the timber option is usually the safer planning choice.
