## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need planning permission to replace a Bournville bay window?**
Bournville sits inside a conservation area with additional Village Trust design covenants. Like-for-like replacement in matching materials is usually acceptable, but a change in frame material or bar pattern normally requires consent.

**Why is bay roof condition so important on a 1930s Birmingham semi?**
Original bay roofs are typically small pitched tile or lead-flat structures that have received almost no maintenance for 90 years. A window swap that ignores a failing bay roof will leak within a couple of winters, so a proper installer will price roof works alongside.

**Can a 1930s semi bay take slim double glazing at Part L rating?**
Yes: 14mm slim units on a warm-edge spacer typically deliver Uw around 1.4 W/m²K, meeting Building Regs Part L targets while keeping the original narrow sightlines that suit the Edwardian and 1930s bay profile.
