## Frequently asked questions

**Can I install UPVC windows on a Bournville property?**
Bournville is a conservation area with additional Bournville Village Trust rules layered on top of council planning. UPVC is generally permitted at the rear, and a woodgrain-foil finish is often approved at the front on non-listed streets. The Trust's design guide is the arbiter.

**What is a fair price for a five-window UPVC replacement on a Kings Heath semi?**
Budget £2,500 to £4,000 fully fitted for a typical 1930s three-bed semi with a bay, two flanking casements upstairs, one kitchen window and a landing window, at 2026 Birmingham rates.

**Do I need scaffolding for a Digbeth flat conversion?**
Ground and first-floor replacements on the outer wall are usually reached from a tower or hop-up. Anything above second-floor level typically needs scaffold or a cherry picker, and installers should show that cost on the quote separately.
