## Frequently asked questions

**Can I replace a Jesmond Victorian bay without touching the roof?**
Usually yes on villas where the bay has its own leaded or slated roof carried on separate corner posts. If the bay carries upper-floor structure (occasional on Osborne Road terraces), you need a structural engineer sign-off before removing the old frames.

**How much for a 1930s Gosforth semi bay?**
A three-panel splayed UPVC bay comes in around £1,360 to £2,400 fitted. Timber-effect UPVC or full timber for a conservation-street Jesmond bay runs £2,600 to £3,230. Reinforced posts and a new tiled bay roof push toward the upper end.

**Are heritage patterns available?**
Yes. Newcastle installers on the panel offer applied Georgian bars, Victorian upper-light patterns and integrated leaded lights — matching original detailing is usually essential in Jesmond and Grainger Town.
