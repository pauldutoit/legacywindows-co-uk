## Frequently asked questions

**Do Tyneside flats need special treatment?**
Yes — the pair of upper and lower front doors and matching windows read as one composition from the street, so replacing only one flat's windows can leave the whole property looking mismatched. Good Newcastle installers will point this out and quote for coordinated replacement where the neighbour is willing.

**Can I put UPVC on a Grainger Town or Jesmond terrace?**
Grainger Town's Georgian frontages are heavily listed — UPVC is essentially never approved on street-facing elevations. Jesmond has multiple conservation areas with Article 4 directions, so timber or timber-effect composite is usually required. Rear elevations often have more latitude.

**Typical Gosforth or Heaton semi prices?**
A full 8-10 window replacement usually lands £3,700 to £6,900 fitted. Individual apertures come in £380 to £765 depending on size, opener count and glass spec.
