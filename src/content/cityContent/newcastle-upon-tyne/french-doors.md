## Frequently asked questions

**Can I fit French doors on a Tyneside flat rear yard?**
Yes, and it is one of the most common Newcastle jobs. The opening is usually a standard 1.5-1.8m wide, ideal for a French door set with fixed side lights. Building Regs still apply — U-value, safety glass and PAS 24 lock spec must be met.

**French, patio or bifolds for a typical Gosforth extension?**
Openings under 2m suit French doors. 2.0-2.4m works for two-panel sliders. Above 3m — common in Gosforth and Ponteland kitchen-diner projects — three or four-panel aluminium bifolds are the norm. Threshold height and sightlines should be quoted explicitly.

**Typical Newcastle prices?**
UPVC French doors £1,105 to £1,700 fitted; aluminium sliders £1,850 to £2,400; three-panel aluminium bifolds £2,350 to £2,720. Timber French sets for LBC jobs push toward £2,150 upwards.
