**Can I fit double-glazed sashes in a Southampton conservation area?**
Usually yes, provided the profile, glazing bar layout and horns match the original. Slim units are almost always acceptable in the fifteen conservation zones, but full 24 mm units on street elevations are usually refused. Bassett Green is one of the stricter areas.

**How much does a full box sash restoration cost versus replacement?**
