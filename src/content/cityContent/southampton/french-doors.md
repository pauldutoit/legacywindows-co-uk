## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need planning permission in Southampton?**
Not usually if they replace an existing opening or are added to a rear wall under permitted development. Article 4 directions in the Old Town or Bassett Green conservation areas remove those rights, so check with Southampton City Council before you commit. Front and side elevations always merit a call to planning.

**What is the difference between French doors, sliding patio doors and bifolds?**
French doors are the traditional two-leaf option with a central mullion or a slim closer bead. Sliders offer a wider glass area on a fixed rail, and bifolds fold back to give the widest opening. French doors typically cost the least of the three.

**Should I specify tempered or laminated glass?**
Both meet Part K on impact safety. Laminated glass adds security and stays intact if broken, and it is standard on our panel for ground-floor Southampton installations.
