## Frequently asked questions

**Does a bay window replacement need Building Regulations sign-off?**
Yes if the bay is load-bearing. On many Southampton Victorian terraces the wall above sits on the bay head, so a lintel and structural calculation is required. FENSA covers the glazing, and a separate building notice covers the structural work.

**Can I change a square bay to a splayed bay?**
Only with planning approval, since the footprint alters the elevation. In a conservation area or on an Article 4 street the answer is almost always no. Like-for-like replacements are far quicker to approve.

**What colour options work best on a Southampton terrace?**
White remains most common, but heritage cream and anthracite grey are increasingly requested in Portswood and Bassett. All three come with the same guarantee.
