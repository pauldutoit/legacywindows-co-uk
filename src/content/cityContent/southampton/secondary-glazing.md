## Frequently asked questions

**Will secondary glazing cut aircraft noise from Southampton Airport?**
Yes, provided the cavity between the primary and secondary pane is at least 100 mm and the secondary unit uses 6 mm acoustic laminated glass. Our panel typically specifies 150 to 200 mm cavities for homes under the SO18 approach.

**Do I need Listed Building Consent for secondary glazing?**
Usually no, because the original frame is untouched. Southampton City Council conservation officers commonly recommend secondary glazing exactly for that reason. It is worth a quick call to confirm before you order.

**Can secondary glazing hit Part L on its own?**
On a single-glazed original frame, adding secondary glazing pulls the assembly Uw down to around 1.8 W/m2K, which counts as compliant improvement work under Part L 2022 for retained fabric.
