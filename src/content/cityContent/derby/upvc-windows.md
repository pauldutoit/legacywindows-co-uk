## Frequently asked questions

**Can I fit UPVC in Little Chester or the Derby historic core?**
Little Chester and parts of the city-centre conservation area apply tighter rules on street-facing frames. UPVC is often refused on visible elevations of listed or locally-listed properties. Check with Derby City Council planning before ordering.

**Do UPVC windows meet Building Regulations Part L?**
Yes, provided the whole-window U-value is 1.4 W/m²K or better and Part F trickle ventilation is included where the previous window had it. Every FENSA installer we shortlist certifies this at handover.
