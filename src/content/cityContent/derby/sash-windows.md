## Frequently asked questions

**Is Little Chester a conservation area?**
Yes. Little Chester is one of Derby's oldest conservation areas, protecting Roman and medieval remains along with Georgian streetscapes. Any window change on a listed or locally-listed property requires consent from Derby City Council.

**How much does draught-proofing cost versus full replacement?**
Draught-proofing a working sash usually costs £250 to £450 per window and cuts infiltration by 60 to 80 percent. A full painted-timber replacement typically starts around £900. Many Derby owners phase the work over several years.

**Can slim double glazing be fitted to an original Georgian sash?**
Usually yes, if the sash box has enough depth to accept a 12-14mm sealed unit. Conservation officers at Derby City Council generally accept slim units when horns and glazing bar profiles match the original.
