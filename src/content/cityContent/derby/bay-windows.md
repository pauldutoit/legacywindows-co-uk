## Frequently asked questions

**Do bay windows need a new lintel when replaced?**
Not always. If the existing steel or timber lintel is sound, it can be retained. Corner posts and head plates carrying part of the load above are the more common issue on Derby Victorian terraces and interwar semis.

**Can I convert a square bay to a bow window?**
Yes, but changing the shape alters the external appearance, which may need planning permission in a conservation area. On unlisted homes outside protected streets, it usually falls under permitted development.

**How long does a bay replacement take?**
A typical square or canted bay is fitted in one to two days once made to measure. Lead times from survey to install run four to seven weeks. Scaffolding is normally needed only if the bay carries a first-floor structure above.
