## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent in Derby?**
Usually no, because it is a reversible internal alteration that does not affect the historic fabric. Derby City Council conservation officers occasionally require consent on Grade I or Grade II* properties, so a check before ordering is sensible.

**How much heat loss does secondary glazing save?**
A well-fitted secondary panel typically halves the U-value on a single-glazed sash, from around 5.4 to 2.5 W/m²K. It also cuts draughts, which usually matters more day-to-day than the U-value figure alone.

**Will it eliminate condensation on my original sashes?**
Condensation often shifts to the outer pane once secondary is fitted, because the room-side air no longer meets a cold single-glazed surface. Trickle ventilation on the outer sash keeps the cavity dry.
