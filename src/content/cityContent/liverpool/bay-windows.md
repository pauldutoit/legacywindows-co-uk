**Does replacing a bay in the Sefton Park conservation area need permission?**
Sefton Park sits inside a Liverpool City Council conservation area with Article 4 Directions on several avenues. Like-for-like replacement in matching materials is usually acceptable, but a change of frame material or facet count requires a planning application.
