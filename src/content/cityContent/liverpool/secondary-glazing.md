## Frequently asked questions

**Does secondary glazing need Listed Building Consent on Rodney Street?**
Almost always yes on Rodney Street, since most properties are Grade II listed. Liverpool City Council heritage officers recognise internal secondary units as reversible and low-impact, so consent is usually granted more readily than for altering the primary sash.

**Will secondary glazing meet Part L on a listed Canning Street house?**
Listed buildings are largely exempt from Part L where full compliance would harm the historic fabric. Secondary glazing typically brings the combined unit down to around Uw 1.7 W/m²K, the accepted heritage benchmark.

**How much noise reduction can I expect on a Sefton Park mansion flat?**
