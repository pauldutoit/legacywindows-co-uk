## Frequently asked questions

**Do French doors need a Building Notice in Liverpool?**
Replacing a door on an existing opening is covered by FENSA or CERTASS self-certification. Widening the opening or removing a load-bearing wall requires a Building Notice submitted to Liverpool City Council building control, with structural calculations for the new lintel.

**Are French doors permitted on a Woolton village conservation street?**
Woolton village is a conservation area with tight external-material rules. Rear French doors are usually accepted in matching timber or aluminium; UPVC is often refused on street-facing openings. Installers in our network check the specific street guidance before quoting.

**How does aluminium compare to UPVC on an Aigburth back extension?**
