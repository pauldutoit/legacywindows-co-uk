## Frequently asked questions

**Do sash windows on Rodney Street need Listed Building Consent?**
Almost always yes: most Rodney Street houses are individually Grade II listed and sit inside the Canning conservation area, so any change to the sash requires Listed Building Consent from Liverpool City Council, even for slim double glazing.

**Can I refurbish rather than replace a Toxteth Victorian sash?**
Yes, and often it is the right answer: where timber is sound and only cords, brushes and beads have failed, refurbishment plus slim double glazing preserves the original glass. Liverpool joiners quote this at 40 to 60 per cent of a full replacement.

**Are UPVC vertical sliders acceptable in the Sefton Park conservation area?**
Non-listed properties outside the strictest streets can usually accept UPVC vertical sliders with a woodgrain-foil finish, but timber remains the safer planning choice on any street with an Article 4 Direction.
